# Welcome to the Knowledge Base

This is the Wise Owl Technologies knowledge base: short, practical articles that explain a problem, show the fix, and cover why it works.

## What you'll find here

- **[Microsoft](/kb/category/microsoft)**: Windows Server, Active Directory, Microsoft 365 and Azure.
- **[VMware](/kb/category/vmware)**: ESXi and vCenter troubleshooting.
- **[Networking](/kb/category/networking)**: DNS, DHCP and connectivity.

## How articles are written

Each article follows the same pattern so you can find answers quickly:

1. **Symptoms**: what you're seeing, including exact error text.
2. **Cause**: why it happens.
3. **Resolution**: numbered steps with copy-and-paste commands.
4. **Verify**: how to confirm it's fixed.

:::tip
Use the search box or the sidebar to jump straight to your topic. Found a mistake or have a better fix? Let me know.
:::
